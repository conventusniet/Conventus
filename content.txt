================================================================================

SECTION A: UNITED NATIONS COMMITTEES
1. United Nations Security Council (UNSC)
   - Description: The UNSC holds primary responsibility for maintaining 
     international peace and security, simulating critical crisis management.
   - Objectives / Agenda:
     * Address urgent threats to international peace.
     * Authorize peacekeeping operations.
     * Recommend solutions to international conflicts.
   - Expected Outcomes: Delegates will simulate real-time crisis scenarios and 
     collaboratively draft resolutions aimed at preserving global security.

2. United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC)
   - Description: Dedicated to promoting and protecting human rights globally, 
     addressing urgent violations, and fostering international cooperation.
   - Objectives / Agenda:
     * Address urgent human rights violations.
     * Promote universal respect for human rights.
     * Enhance international cooperation in protecting human rights.
   - Expected Outcomes: Delegates will draft resolutions and action plans aimed 
     at rectifying human rights abuses and promoting global justice.

SECTION B: INDIAN COMMITTEES
1. All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM)
   - Description: Simulates India's dynamic political landscape, where political 
     representatives debate, negotiate, and collaborate on key national issues.
   - Objectives / Agenda:
     * Simulate India's domestic political environment.
     * Debate critical national socio-economic and political issues.
     * Encourage cross-party collaboration.
   - Expected Outcomes: Delegates form alliances, draft policy bills, and defend 
     political stances echoing real-world national discourse.

SECTION C: INTERNATIONAL PRESS (IP)
1. Journalism: Report on committee proceedings, write analytical and hard-hitting 
   articles, and document conference developments.
2. Photography: Capture key conference moments, create visual documentation, and 
   build the event's photographic portfolio.
3. Videography: Create creative, artistic commentary, and highlight debates
